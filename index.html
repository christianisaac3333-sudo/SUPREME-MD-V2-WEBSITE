04 / TELEGRAM

TELEGRAM CONTROL

/startStart the shared bot
/link CODELink your website account
/menuShow Telegram commands
/alive · /ping · /healthCheck bot and API status
/statusCheck your WhatsApp session
/pair COUNTRYCODERequest a private pairing code
/connectStart QR connection
/disconnectDisconnect your own session
/calc · /title · /trimUse the utility commands
/profile · /accountView your linked account
/admin · /statsAdmin-only platform tools

Telegram commands require a private chat and a linked SUPREME MD V2 website account.

Use a dot prefix on WhatsApp and a slash prefix on Telegram. Never share account passwords, QR codes, pairing codes or Telegram link codes.
